# Administrator Guide: Overview

The FOSSBilling Administrator Portal (`http://localhost:3000`) gives operators full command over customers, subscriptions, automated service provisioners, payment gateways, and system diagnostics.

---

## 📊 Executive Dashboard

The dashboard provides real-time visibility into key financial and operational metrics:
- **MRR / ARR Analytics:** Monthly and Annual Recurring Revenue trends.
- **Active Subscriptions:** Live counts of hosting plans, domains, and license orders.
- **Pending Actions:** Unpaid invoices, open support tickets, and pending manual transactions.
- **System Health:** CPU, memory, database latency, and background worker status.

---

## 📑 Core Navigation Modules

- **👥 Clients:** Manage client accounts, profile data, credit balances, and custom login sessions.
- **📦 Orders & Subscriptions:** View active, suspended, pending, and canceled services.
- **🧾 Invoices & Billing:** Issue manual invoices, process refunds, record payments, and export PDFs.
- **🎧 Support Tickets:** Ticket routing, staff assignments, internal notes, and canned responses.
- **🏷️ Products & Catalog:** Configure hosting plans, domain pricing matrices, addons, and custom fields.
- **⚡ Servers:** Add and monitor remote cPanel, Plesk, DirectAdmin, HestiaCP, and CWP servers.
- **💳 Payment Gateways:** Configure Stripe, PayPal, Midtrans, and manual bank transfers.
- **🌐 Domains:** TLD pricing rules, registrar credentials, and automated domain renewals.
- **🧩 Extensions & Marketplace:** Install modules, plugins, form builders, and widgets.
- **🛡️ Security & Anti-Spam:** Manage IP blocklists, honeypot settings, and audit logs.
