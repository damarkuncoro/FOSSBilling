# Themes, Embeddable Widgets & SEO

FOSSBilling allows visual theme switching, dynamic embeddable widgets, and automated search engine optimization (SEO).

---

## 🎨 Themes & Custom Styling

- **Dark & Light Modes:** Both Administrator and Client portals feature sleek modern themes with tailored color palettes, glassmorphism, and instant mode switching.
- **Directional Support (LTR & RTL):** Layouts automatically adapt for right-to-left languages like Arabic.

---

## 🧩 Embeddable Widgets (`/embed-widgets`)

Administrators can generate embeddable HTML/JavaScript snippets to place on external landing pages or marketing websites:
- **Order Buttons:** Instant checkout buttons for specific hosting packages.
- **Domain Search Bar:** Standalone domain lookup widget that redirects to the cart.
- **Login / Client Bar:** Quick customer portal status bar.
- **Contact & Support Widget:** Floating helpdesk bubble.

---

## 🔍 SEO & Sitemap Automation (`/seo`)

- **Dynamic Sitemap:** Automatically aggregates active storefront products, news articles, and custom pages at `/sitemap.xml`.
- **Search Engine Pings:** Automatically alerts Google and Bing upon new product announcements.
