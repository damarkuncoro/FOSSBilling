# Company Information

Setting up your company profile is essential for branding your client portal and ensuring invoices contain the correct legal and contact information.

---

## 🏢 Branding Your Business

In the Admin Portal, navigate to **Settings > Company Information**.

### Essential Fields
- **Business Name:** Your official company or brand name.
- **Contact Email:** The primary email for client inquiries (often used as the 'From' address).
- **Phone Number:** Displayed in the support area and invoice footers.
- **Official Address:** Used for generating legal tax invoices.

---

## 🖼️ Logos and Icons

You can upload separate logos for different display contexts:
- **Light Logo:** For dark backgrounds or sidebar headers.
- **Dark Logo:** For light backgrounds and PDF invoices.
- **Favicon:** The small icon displayed in the browser tab.

---

## 📜 Legal Links

Configure links to your legal documents to ensure compliance:
- **Terms of Service (ToS):** Clients must agree to these during registration.
- **Privacy Policy:** Required for GDPR and other data protection regulations.
- **Service Level Agreement (SLA):** Defines your uptime guarantees and support response times.

---

## ✍️ Email Signature

Define a global email signature that will be appended to all outgoing transactional emails sent by FOSSBilling. You can use HTML to include your logo and links to your social media.
