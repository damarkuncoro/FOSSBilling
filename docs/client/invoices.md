# Invoices, Payments & Deposit Funds

Clients can manage their billing history, settle pending charges, and deposit prepay credits.

---

## 🧾 Managing Invoices

- **Invoice List (`/invoices`):** Displays all invoices with current status (`paid`, `unpaid`, `canceled`, `refunded`).
- **1-Click Settlement:** Open unpaid invoices and pay directly via Credit Card, PayPal, or Midtrans QRIS.
- **Download Official PDF:** Download signed PDF tax invoices with itemized VAT/tax breakdowns.

---

## 💳 Account Deposit Prepay

Clients can deposit funds in advance into their account balance:
1. Click **Deposit Funds** in the client navigation or Invoices page.
2. Select or enter the deposit amount (e.g. `$50.00`).
3. Settle the deposit invoice via your preferred payment gateway.
4. Future recurring renewal invoices will automatically deduct from this balance.
