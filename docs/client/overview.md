# Customer Portal Guide: Overview

The FOSSBilling Customer Portal (`http://localhost:3001`) gives clients a modern, responsive, and seamless interface to browse hosting plans, register domains, pay invoices, manage cloud services, and get technical assistance.

::: tip Detailed Step-by-Step Purchasing Walkthrough
For a complete visual flowchart and purchasing walkthrough, visit the **[Complete Step-by-Step Usage Guide](/guide/step-by-step-guide#phase-5-client-ordering-purchasing-journey)**.
:::

---

## 🚶 The Customer Journey: Step-by-Step

### 1. Registration & Security Onboarding
- Visit the portal and click **Register** or sign up directly during the checkout flow.
- Built-in honeypot algorithms and disposable email filters protect against automated bots without annoying captchas.
- Set currency preference and toggle interface language (with automatic RTL support for Arabic/Hebrew).

### 2. Browsing & Ordering Services
- **[Storefront & Ordering](/client/storefront):** Browse categorized hosting plans, VPS, and cloud packages.
- **Domain Availability Checker:** Real-time lookup for `.com`, `.net`, `.io`, and regional TLDs. Choose between new registration, registrar transfer, or bringing your own domain.
- **Dynamic Configuration Forms:** Configure server hostnames, root passwords, OS distribution (Ubuntu, Debian, AlmaLinux), and optional add-ons.

### 3. Reviewing Cart & Applying Discounts
- Inspect itemized shopping cart items with taxes and discounts applied in real time.
- Enter promotional coupon codes (e.g. `PROMO10`).
- Seamless checkout in 1 click.

### 4. Paying Invoices & Managing Funds
- **[Invoices & Payments](/client/invoices):** Instant payment via Credit/Debit Cards (Stripe), PayPal, Midtrans QRIS/Virtual Accounts, or Bank Wire.
- **Prepay Deposits:** Add funds in advance so recurring subscription invoices are settled automatically without service disruption.
- **Tax Invoices & Receipts:** Download official, signed PDF invoices with tax breakdown.

### 5. Managing Active Services & Single Sign-On (SSO)
- **[Licenses & Downloads](/client/licenses-downloads):** View server IP addresses, nameservers, disk/bandwidth stats, and reset license bindings.
- **Control Panel SSO:** 1-Click login directly into cPanel, HestiaCP, or Plesk without typing credentials.

### 6. Submitting Support Tickets & Getting Help
- **[Helpdesk & Support](/client/support):** Open tickets with departments (Technical, Billing, Sales), set urgency, attach logs/screenshots, and receive real-time updates.
